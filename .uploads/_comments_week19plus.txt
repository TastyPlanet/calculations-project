## 2026.5.21.木（小满）
- 小满, the eighth of the 二十四节气 and the second solar term of summer, falls between May 20 and 22 on the 公历. At this time, nature strikes a delicate balance — a state of approaching fullness without excess. In the north, wheat kernels enter the milk-ripe stage, swollen yet unripe, giving rise to the saying "小满小满，麦粒渐满". In the south, rivers swell with seasonal rains but stop short of overflowing, as captured in the saying "小满小满，江河渐满".<br>The name 小满 itself is a masterstroke of observation and philosophy. It does not denote literal incompleteness but celebrates a state of nearing fulfillment without tipping into excess — a reflection of the ancient wisdom "月满则亏，水满则溢". True abundance, as Chinese tradition teaches, lies not in reaching the peak but in preserving room for growth.<br>This ethos permeates 小满's 三候: 一候苦菜秀 — bitter greens flourish, cleansing internal heat; 二候靡草死 — shade-tender grasses wither under the intensifying yang energy; 三候麦秋至 — wheat enters "grain autumn", a term that hints at harvest even as summer holds sway. Notably, the 二十四节气 do not include a "大满". This absence is deliberate — a cultural safeguard against the principle of 盈满则亏. As 曾国藩 upheld "花未全开月未圆" as life's ideal, so too did 欧阳修 write in his five-character quatrain: "最爱垄头麦，迎风笑落红". Both honor 小满's essence: ripeness deferred, potential preserved.<br>In agriculture, 小满's dual nature shapes regional practices. Northerners guard wheat fields against hot dry winds, while southerners race to transplant rice seedlings. The season's busyness is captured in the saying "小满动三车". Customs echo its core lesson of restraint: farmers worship the 车神, splashing water into fields to pray for abundance without overflow; households eat bitter greens following the belief "小满食苦，一夏不苦"; and in Jiangnan, rituals honor 嫘祖 as silkworm cocoons reach their critical stage.<br>小满 is more than a seasonal marker — it is a parable in miniature. Like wheat bending with nearly ripe kernels, it teaches humility in achievement; like rivers swelling without spilling, it models the grace of restraint. In a world obsessed with "fullness", 小满 whispers a timeless truth: the deepest fulfillment dwells not at the summit, but in the poised grace of "nearly there."

- 近期刷到不少关于极端高温的视频，数据显示全球最热的百座城市里，有95座都来自India. 顺着高温话题往下看，我还留意到了AQI.<br>AQI，全称 Air Quality Index，中文名“空气质量指数”，是用来直观判断空气好坏的通用指标。它整合了PM2.5、沙尘、臭氧等多项污染物的监测数据，换算成0至500的数值。数值越低，空气越洁净；数值越高，空气污染就越严重，对日常出行和身体健康的影响也越大。简单来说，不用看复杂的专业数据，单凭这个数字，就能快速判断当下的空气环境是否适合户外活动。<br>最近一段时间，天气一直阴沉闷热，晴好的天气寥寥无几，气温也持续偏高。翻看全球城市数据时能明显发现，很多出现极端高温的地区，AQI数值也普遍偏高。像阿曼、印度等常年酷热的地区，高温少风、持续暴晒的天气，很容易滋生臭氧污染，扬起沙尘颗粒物，让高温和空气污染同时出现。<br>身处持续闷热的天气里，这种感受格外真切。高温带来身体上的燥热，浑浊的空气又让人倍感压抑，双重影响下，日常体感会差很多。以前我只会关注气温高低，很少留意空气质量，现在慢慢习惯结合AQI一起观察天气变化。气温和空气质量的联动变化，也能直观感受到气候环境的细微改变。频繁的高温、反复的空气浑浊，都是身边最真实的环境信号。关注AQI、留意日常天气，不仅是为了做好自我防护，也能让人更珍惜舒适的气候与洁净的空气，重视日常的环境守护。
